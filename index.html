How do I get my followers_1.html and following.html files from Instagram?
STEP 1

Open Accounts Center

In Instagram Settings or Web, click Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.

STEP 2

Download Information

Click Download your information → Download or transfer information → Choose your profile.

STEP 3

Select Format: HTML

Select Some of your information → check Followers and following. Choose format: HTML, Date range: All time.

STEP 4

Extract & Upload

Unzip the file you receive. Open connections/followers_and_following/ to find followers_1.html and following.html.
